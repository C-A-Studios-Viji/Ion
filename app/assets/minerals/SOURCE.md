# Crystal mineral meshes

Author: iPoly3D
Pack: LowPoly Crystals Pack (28 models)
Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-crystals-pack
Original archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/crystals_0.zip

Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
Retrieved: 29 October 2026

Original STL files in the archive's STL directory are converted from Z-up to
Y-up, centred, grounded and normalized to one unit high. Geometry is preserved;
no new faces or smoothing across crystal facets are introduced. ASCII STL is
used for portability. Materials are supplied by ION at runtime because STL
contains no material or texture data.

These are stylized game representations, not crystallographic reference models.
Reproduce conversions with scripts/import-crystals.mjs and the original pack.
